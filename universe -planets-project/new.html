From Village Dreams to Chennai Streets

I came from a small village with a big dream — to earn money and build a better life. But the city didn’t welcome me easily. This is my story of struggle, survival, and growth in Chennai.

📖 My Survival Journey

1. The First Day in Chennai

When I stepped into Chennai, everything felt new — the sounds of traffic, the smell of food stalls, and people walking fast everywhere. I didn’t know anyone. I had only ₹500 in my pocket and one small bag. That night, I slept near a bus stand because I couldn’t afford a room.

2. Finding Work — and Failing

I started searching for work. I went door to door in shops and hotels, asking “Sir, job iruka?” Some said no. Some didn’t even answer. I tried for delivery work, but they asked for my own bike. I didn’t have one. I even worked one day in a construction site, but they didn’t pay properly. I learned — in Chennai, no one helps if you stop trying.

3. Missing Home & Feeling Lost

Nights were the hardest. I missed my parents, my friends, the calm air of my village. Here, even sleep was a luxury. Sometimes I questioned why I came. But every time I thought to go back, I remembered why I started — I came for a better life.

4. Learning to Survive

Slowly, I started to understand the city. I learned basic Tamil and English to talk with people. I started saving money, skipping unnecessary food, and focused on learning new skills online. I realized — Chennai is not against us, it just tests our patience.

5. My First Success

After months of searching, I got a small part-time job in a delivery company. My first salary was ₹6,000. I couldn’t stop smiling when I sent money home. It wasn’t much, but it was the start of something big. That day, I felt like I earned respect — not just money.

6. What Chennai Taught Me

  • Never depend fully on anyone — learn to stand on your own.
  • Even failure teaches you something important.
  • Money will come, but experience is more valuable.
  • Respect every worker — every job has dignity.
